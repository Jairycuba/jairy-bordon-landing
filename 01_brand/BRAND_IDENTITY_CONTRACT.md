# Brand Identity Contract

The phase-specific source of truth is `01_brand/PHASE_0_IDENTITY_AND_POSITIONING_CONTRACT.md`.

## Approved name

**Jairy Bordon**

Written exactly this way. Pronunciation: **Hairy Bordón**. The monogram is **JB**, never HB.

## Positioning

Jairy Bordon is an independent software developer and digital-systems consultant who turns complex ideas, workflows and business processes into useful software.

CubaMía Catering is an independent business and must not be presented as part of Jairy Bordon. LifeOptions Academy remains separate as the financial-education and trading brand.

The positioning is intentionally broader than “websites for small businesses.” It can include:

- Web applications.
- Custom software.
- Automation.
- System integrations.
- MCP integrations.
- Agent-based systems.
- Specialized software, including personal trading projects.
- Discovery, requirements clarification and prototype definition.

## Approved logo reference

Use `06_assets/JB_logo_reference.jpeg` as the visual reference. The approved form is the clean navy-and-turquoise JB monogram on a white background.

Do not replace it with:

- A dark square containing a white B.
- Separate J and B letters.
- A cropped B.
- A distorted or stretched version.
- The letters HB.

## Visual direction

- Modern, technical and approachable.
- Navy blue and turquoise as primary colors.
- Strong readability and generous spacing.
- Professional without appearing distant or corporate.
- The monogram must remain recognizable at small sizes.

## Design acceptance criteria

Any future card, flyer, landing page or social asset must be compared against the approved logo reference before approval. A design is not accepted merely because it has a “JB” label; it must use the correct complete monogram.
